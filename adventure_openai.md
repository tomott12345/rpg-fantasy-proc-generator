# Quest Type: Dungeon Delving

## Region
**Sadri Desert**

---

## Adventure Theme
Dungeon Delving

---

## Quest Summary
- **Type:** Dungeon Delving  
- **Region:** Sadri Desert  
- **Twist:** Time flows strangely within the ruin  
- **Complication:** A rival faction interferes  
- **Reward Tier:** Standard

---

## Adventure Hooks
- **Hook A:** Lead from survivor testimony of a talking guardian.  
- **Hook B:** Rival faction offers payment to capture the monster.  
- **Hook C:** Local tomb-robbers vanish; one returns cursed.

---

## Key Locations

### Settlement: Glitterhold Forest City
- A relic-trade hub built atop ancient ruins and fog-laden streets.

### Dungeon: The Howling Crypt
- Depth: 3 levels  
- Challenge Rating Guess: 3  
- Description: A cursed cliffside tomb where runes flicker with green fire.

---

## Monster Encounters

### The Bound Guardian
- **Type:** Undead (Sapient)  
- **CR:** 3  
- **Description:** Once a paladin, now a skeletal warden bound by spectral chains.  
- **Abilities:**  
  - *Spectral Chains (Recharge 5–6):* 10-ft AoE restrain, DC 13 Strength save.  
  - *Voice of the Fallen:* Speaks Common; remembers oaths.  
- **Behavior:** Will parley if approached respectfully; desires release.

### Scarlet Covenant Agents
- **Type:** Humanoid Cultists  
- **CR:** 2  
- **Description:** Necromantic zealots seeking to enslave the guardian.  
- **Abilities:**  
  - *Necrotic Bolt:* 1d8+2 necrotic damage, range 60 ft.  
  - *Fanatical Zeal*

## Resolution Options
The fate of Riftmarch hinges on the party’s choices:

1. **Slay the guardian and claim the relics.**  
2. **Free the soul by invoking the true name at the altar.**  
3. **Strike a bargain with the Scarlet Covenant.**

---

## Notes for the DM
- Time flows strangely inside *The Howling Crypt*, affecting spell durations and rest cycles unpredictably.  
- A rival faction, the Scarlet Covenant cultists, actively interferes, complicating the mission.  
- Use the procedural tables for traps, relics, dialogue, events, and random encounters to keep the adventure dynamic and engaging.  
- The Bound Guardian may be convinced to parley rather than fight outright, opening alternative resolutions.  
- Glitterhold Forest City offers opportunities for gathering information, trading relics, and encountering NPCs tied to the quest.  

Prepare to adapt based on player choices and their approach to the mysterious, cursed tomb!
